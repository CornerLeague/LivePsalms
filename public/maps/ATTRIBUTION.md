# Map Image Attribution

Provenance for every map image in `public/maps/`. Historical ("then") maps are
shipped as genuinely public-domain works; modern ("now") maps are **not yet
shipped** (see below). All shipped images remain subject to **human verification**
of geographic accuracy before production.

A reviewer must confirm, for each shipped image: (1) license accuracy and
(2) geographic appropriateness for the region/tab.

---

## Shipped — historical ("then") maps

Both "then" maps are plates from George Adam Smith's *Atlas of the Historical
Geography of the Holy Land* (London: Hodder and Stoughton, 1915) — a pre-1931
publication, public domain in the US and (author d. 1942) in the UK under the
70-year rule. The originals were ~5–7 MB; each has been **downscaled to 1600px
on the long edge and re-encoded** for web delivery (full-res originals remain on
Wikimedia Commons via the file pages below).

## NOT shipped — modern ("now") maps

The modern "Today" maps are **intentionally absent**. The only candidate sourced
so far was Wikimedia's *"Israel and occupied territories"* map (ChrisO, derived
from a UN Cartographic Section map). It was **rejected** for two reasons:

1. **Editorial framing** — it foregrounds modern political/conflict designations
   ("occupied territories", Gaza/West Bank boundaries), which is inappropriate
   for a devotional Bible-study context.
2. **License uncertainty** — "UN-derived ⇒ public domain" is not a safe
   inference; the UN generally asserts rights over its cartographic works, and
   the derivative's status was not independently confirmed.

Until a **neutral, openly-licensed** modern reference map (e.g. an OpenStreetMap-
derived physical/terrain map with proper attribution, or a clearly CC0/CC-BY
Wikimedia file) is sourced and cleared, the `now` binaries are absent. The
registry's `now` entries carry `license: 'Pending human review'` and an honest
"not yet sourced" attribution, and the Today tab renders the graceful
"Map image unavailable." fallback. No fabricated provenance ships.
