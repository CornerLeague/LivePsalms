**Passage:** Romans 8 · **Question:** Is it wrong to be angry with God about this?

## Reply

No—the Scriptures supplied make room for bringing this anger before the Lord. Psalm 13 does not hide its protest: “How long, O LORD?” Its anguish is addressed to God, not kept safely away from him. Your note’s resistance to an easy use of “good” fits Romans 8’s own setting: Paul speaks of creation and believers groaning while redemption is still unseen. Romans 8:28 is not presented as a name for present pain; its next sentence names the good toward which God’s purpose moves—being conformed to the Son’s image. And where words fail, the Spirit meets weakness with intercession. Scripture therefore holds honest anguish and enduring hope together, rather than asking you to call suffering good before you can see its end.
