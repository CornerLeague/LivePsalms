## Reply

No—Scripture does not treat honest anguish before the Lord as forbidden. Psalm 13 begins, “How long, O LORD?” and even dares to name the felt absence of God. Your anger is not hidden by refusing to write it down; the psalm gives it language before God. Romans 8 likewise does not call suffering good. It describes creation and God’s children as groaning, while the Spirit meets weakness with intercession when words fail. And the “good” of Romans 8:28 is clarified as conformity to Christ, not a claim that the pain itself is good or easily explained. Read against what you wrote, your protest belongs beside the groaning Paul describes—not outside God’s hearing.
