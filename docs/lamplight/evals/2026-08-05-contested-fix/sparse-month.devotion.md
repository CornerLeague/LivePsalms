# sparse-month · devotion

_Two short notes in a whole month. Tests that thin input produces restraint rather than padding._

Dana — when a week leaves few words, even taking a seat at the back can carry more weight than it appears.

Matthew 11:28

Come to Me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest.

Jesus’ invitation in Matthew 11:28 does not begin with an account of why the weary are tired or with a demand that they explain themselves. It begins with “Come to Me.” Read beside your brief account of a long week and your place at the early service, the passage makes room for an approach that is not polished or conspicuous. Sitting at the back may reflect a desire simply to be present when energy for much else is thin. The rest Jesus names is given by him; it is not presented as a reward for having the right words. Matthew 11:28 holds weariness and welcome together.

What does it feel like to be invited to Jesus without first having to explain the weight you carry?
