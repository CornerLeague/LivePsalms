# long-vault · devotion

_A dense month with many entries. Tests selection rather than summary._

Mara — the noise has not disappeared, yet the list of fears has begun to lose items, and even others have noticed a change you cannot easily explain.

Philippians 4:6

Be anxious for nothing, but in everything, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God.

Philippians 4:6 does not pretend anxiety has no objects; it names “everything” as material to bring before God. The passage turns on exchange: fear need not remain only an inward inventory when it can become prayer, specific petition, and thanksgiving. Read beside your shortened list, this suggests that the change may be less about mastering every uncertainty than about where those uncertainties are being carried. Thanksgiving is not denial of what is loud or difficult; it remembers that God is present amid the same circumstances. The calm another person noticed may be hard to account for because this verse directs attention away from self-explanation and toward God, who receives what is named.

Which remaining fear feels most ready to be named plainly before God?
