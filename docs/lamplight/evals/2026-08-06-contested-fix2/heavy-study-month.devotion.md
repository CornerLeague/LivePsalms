Priya — the psalm’s final note does not erase its plea; it leaves desire facing the long interval before what it seeks is seen.

Psalms 27:14

Wait patiently for the LORD; be strong and courageous. Wait patiently for the LORD!

The repeated command in Psalms 27:14 is striking precisely because you have noticed the psalm move from confidence into pleading. Its ending is not arrival, nor a denial of the need voiced in the middle; it is a resolve to remain turned toward the LORD. Read beside your attention to dwelling, waiting begins to look less like an empty hallway and more like staying before the One desired. The passage turns on repetition: “Wait patiently for the LORD” frames strength and courage, suggesting that courage is not merely forceful action but endurance in relation to Him. Your question about why waiting receives the final word may be asking whether desire can remain faithful without immediate resolution. This verse leaves that tension open, yet locates it before the LORD rather than outside His house.

What changes if waiting is understood as remaining oriented toward the LORD, rather than merely postponing arrival?
