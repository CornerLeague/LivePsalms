# sparse-month · devotion

_Two short notes in a whole month. Tests that thin input produces restraint rather than padding._

Dana — some weeks leave only a few words, and even worship can feel like a place to sit at the edge rather than explain what is heavy.

Matthew 11:28

Come to Me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest.

Jesus addresses people carrying weariness and burdens, not people who have first found language for them. Read beside your sparse note after a long week, the passage makes room for what cannot yet be narrated. The early service and the back row suggest a desire to be present without needing to be conspicuous. Matthew 11:28 turns on Christ’s invitation: rest is located in coming to Him, rather than in producing a stronger account of oneself. Scripture does not minimize the weight; it names it, then places Jesus Himself before the burdened. The few words you had may be enough to bring into His presence.

What part of this week feels hardest to name, yet remains present before Jesus?
