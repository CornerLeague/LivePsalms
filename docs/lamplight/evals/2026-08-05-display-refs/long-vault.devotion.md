# long-vault · devotion

_A dense month with many entries. Tests selection rather than summary._

Mara — beneath the louder rooms and crowded days, one fear has quietly lost some of its claim on you.

Philippians 4:6

Be anxious for nothing, but in everything, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God.

Philippians 4:6 does not pretend anxiety is imaginary; it redirects its movement. The passage turns on “in everything”: the fears that made it onto your list, the friction that required a late apology, and the new job’s noise are not too small or too tangled to be brought before God. Prayer and petition give fear an address; thanksgiving keeps the request joined to remembrance rather than to dread alone. Read against your notes, the shorter list is worth noticing—not as proof that you have mastered anxiety, but as a sign that what is named can be carried into God’s presence. The calm someone else noticed may be less an answer you possess than a posture being formed.

What fear or unfinished tension feels most ready to be named before God today?
