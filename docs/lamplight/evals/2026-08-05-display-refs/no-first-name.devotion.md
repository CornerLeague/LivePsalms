Lamentations 3:23

They are new every morning; great is Your faithfulness!

Lamentations speaks this line from within grief, not from a trouble-free horizon. Its claim is not that mornings arrive painless, but that the Lord’s mercies meet people again where they actually wake. Read beside your notes, the passage holds together a broken night, prayer for neighbours, and a day that softened as it went. The turn is subtle: fatigue did not close the door on love, and the day’s later gentleness did not erase the morning’s weight. “New every morning” names God’s faithfulness as fresh provision, not a demand that you begin strong. Lamentations 3:23 leaves room for both weariness and gratitude.
