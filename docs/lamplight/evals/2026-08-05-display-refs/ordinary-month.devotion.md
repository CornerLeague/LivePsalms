# ordinary-month · devotion

_An unremarkable month. The hardest case: nothing dramatic to hang a devotion on._

Sam — the day’s ordinary edges have begun to disclose more than productivity: a prepared meal, a held moment of light, a return to the first page.

Psalms 16:6

The lines of my boundary have fallen in pleasant places; surely my inheritance is delightful.

Psalms 16:6 speaks of boundaries not as deprivation but as an inheritance received. The verse turns on that reversal: what marks out a life can become a place of delight. Read beside your notes, the passage gives weight to the small contours of a day—running, cooking, pausing before light on a kitchen wall, beginning a book again more slowly. None is spectacular, yet each belongs within the “pleasant places” the psalm names. The slower return is especially telling: attention can make familiar ground newly inhabitable. Scripture does not deny limits; it imagines them as places where gratitude may recognize what has been given.

Where have the ordinary boundaries of your day felt unexpectedly like a gift?
