# grief-month · devotion

_A month shaped by a death. Tests that the voice accompanies rather than explains._

Ruth — grief has altered the ordinary rituals of home, while an anger without a clear target keeps asking to be acknowledged.

Psalms 34:18

The LORD is near to the brokenhearted; He saves the contrite in spirit.

The psalm does not make brokenheartedness a failure to overcome; it names it as the very place where the LORD is near (Psalms 34:18). Read beside the unfinished reading at the service and the extra cup of tea, its nearness meets loss not as an explanation but as presence within what has been torn open. The verse also joins a broken heart to a spirit brought low. That may give language to anger that has no single object: sorrow can resist the emptiness death leaves behind. Scripture holds both without pretending the house is not changed. The LORD’s saving nearness is spoken into the valley of bereavement, not around it.

What part of this changed home most clearly tells you what you are grieving?
