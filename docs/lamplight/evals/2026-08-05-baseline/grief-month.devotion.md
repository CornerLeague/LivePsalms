# grief-month · devotion

_A month shaped by a death. Tests that the voice accompanies rather than explains._

Ruth — grief has altered the smallest rituals, leaving absence at the table and anger without a clear address.

psa 23:4

Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for You are with me; Your rod and Your staff, they comfort me.

Psalm 23:4 does not rush the walker out of the valley; it names the path as one that passes through death’s shadow. Read beside the unfinished reading at the service and the second cup of tea, the verse makes room for grief that interrupts speech and rearranges ordinary habits. Its center is not an explanation for loss, but “You are with me.” The rod and staff are shepherd’s tools: images of guarding and guiding when the way is dark. Your anger, too, need not be made tidy before it can be brought into this valley. Scripture holds the shadow and the Shepherd’s presence in the same sentence.

Where does the valley feel most tangible in the ordinary rooms of your house?
