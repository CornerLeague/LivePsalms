# long-vault · devotion

_A dense month with many entries. Tests selection rather than summary._

Mara — amid a louder life, the most revealing change may be that fear has begun to lose some of its inventory.

php 4:6

Be anxious for nothing, but in everything, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God.

The passage turns on movement: anxiety is not treated as a private problem to master, but as material to bring before God. “In everything” gives even the fears that made your list a place in prayer; “with thanksgiving” keeps petition from becoming a catalogue of threats alone. Read against what you have written, the shorter list and the calmer impression others notice may point less to having every pressure resolved than to fear no longer occupying the whole field. php 4:6 does not deny the loudness of work or the strain of relationships. It places them before the Lord as requests, where gratitude and need can stand together.

What fear, now small enough to name, do you want to place before the Lord?
