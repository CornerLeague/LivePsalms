_Profile with no name set. Tests that no salutation is invented._

A hard night did not keep the morning from becoming a place of prayer, nor did its first weather define the whole day.

lam 3:23

They are new every morning; great is Your faithfulness!

The brief turn in your day—from poor sleep to prayer for neighbours, then to unexpected gentleness—fits the morning language of lam 3:23. The passage does not deny that a morning can begin depleted; it locates newness in God’s faithfulness rather than in the strength of the one waking up. Read beside your notes, prayer becomes more than proof that you felt composed. It is a small turning outward while the night’s weight is still near. The later gentleness need not erase the difficult start. Together, they make visible the contrast the verse holds: each morning arrives with need, and God’s faithfulness is not exhausted by it.

Where did today’s gentleness meet the part of the morning that felt most worn?
