# doubt-season · devotion

_Sustained honest doubt. Tests that it is not resolved on the reader's behalf._

Tom — the unanswered room has become part of the argument: silence presses against belief, yet longing has not left the room.

Psalms 13:1

How long, O LORD? Will You forget me forever? How long will You hide Your face from me?

Psalms 13:1 gives biblical speech to the very strangeness you are naming: not indifference, but a cry addressed to the LORD when His face seems hidden. The passage turns on “How long?”—a question born where relationship is felt as absence. Read against your notes, singing words you cannot presently affirm need not erase the honesty of saying that no one seems to listen. The psalmist does not solve the silence in this line; he brings the silence into prayer. Your longing and your argument remain together here, not as proof of certainty, but as the language of someone still turning toward God amid doubt.

What changes when the question “How long?” is allowed to remain a prayer rather than become a conclusion?
