Lamentations 3:23

They are new every morning; great is Your faithfulness!

Lamentations speaks from a place of devastation, not from an easy optimism. Its claim that God’s mercies are new each morning meets a morning that began depleted and yet still made room for prayer. The passage turns on God’s faithfulness rather than the steadiness of our sleep, mood, or circumstances. Read beside your notes, the prayer for neighbours appears before the day became gentler; it was not a reward for feeling well, but a small act of attention amid weariness. The later change in the day does not erase the hard night. It does, however, keep the night from having the last word. Lamentations 3:23 names that horizon: God’s faithfulness can meet a day at its beginning and unfold within it.

Where did the day’s gentleness become visible after its difficult beginning?
