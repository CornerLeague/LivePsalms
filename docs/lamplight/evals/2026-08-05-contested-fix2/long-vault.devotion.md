# long-vault · devotion

_A dense month with many entries. Tests selection rather than summary._

Mara — beneath the louder days, something in you has begun to loosen its grip on fear, even before you can fully explain the change.

Philippians 4:6

Be anxious for nothing, but in everything, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God.

Philippians 4:6 does not deny that there are real requests to carry; it turns on where those requests are brought. Paul places anxiety beside prayer, petition, and thanksgiving—not as a demand to manufacture calm, but as an invitation to bring the actual matter before God. Read against your shrinking fear list, this may illuminate why “calmer” has become visible to someone else before it became easy to name. The passage makes room for specificity: each concern can become a request, and thanksgiving keeps that request within remembered grace. Your notes suggest fear has not been ignored; it has been faced, named, and found less absolute.

Which remaining fear feels most ready to be named plainly before God?
