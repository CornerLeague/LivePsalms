Lamentations speaks into communal ruin, yet it locates hope not in an easy beginning but in the Lord’s faithful mercies meeting each morning. Read beside your notes, the passage turns on that distinction: poor sleep was real, and prayer for your neighbours still rose from within it. The later gentleness does not erase the hard start; it makes room to notice that a day need not remain governed by its first ache. “They are new every morning” (Lamentations 3:23) names the Lord’s steadiness when our own strength is thin. Your brief record holds both weariness and an unexpected softening of the day.
