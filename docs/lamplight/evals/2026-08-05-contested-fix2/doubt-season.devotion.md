# doubt-season · devotion

_Sustained honest doubt. Tests that it is not resolved on the reader's behalf._

Tom — the hardest part may not be the doubt itself, but the strange persistence of longing when prayer seems to meet only the ceiling.

Psalms 13:1

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. How long, O LORD? Will You forget me forever? How long will You hide Your face from me?

Psalms 13:1 does not tidy silence into a quick reassurance. David addresses the LORD precisely by asking whether he has been forgotten and whether God’s face is hidden. The passage turns on that tension: the complaint is still spoken to God, not merely spoken about him. Read against your notes, singing words you cannot presently affirm and naming the strangeness of longing may be closer to this psalm’s candor than either forced certainty or indifference. Tom, the question “How long?” gives your unanswered prayer a biblical grammar. It leaves the ache exposed before the LORD without pretending that feeling nothing settles what is true.

What changes when your question is addressed to the LORD rather than left alone inside the silence?
