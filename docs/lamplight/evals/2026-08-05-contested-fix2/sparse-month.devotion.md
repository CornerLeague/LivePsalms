# sparse-month · devotion

_Two short notes in a whole month. Tests that thin input produces restraint rather than padding._

Dana — At the end of a long week, even taking a seat at the back can carry more weight than it shows.

Matthew 11:28

Come to Me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest.

Jesus’ invitation in Matthew 11:28 is addressed not to people who have arranged themselves well, but to those who are weary and carrying weight. The passage turns on His words, “Come to Me”: rest is not presented as a reward for having more to say or more strength to bring. Read beside your brief account of a long week and your place at the early service, the back row need not be outside the reach of this invitation. You came into the room where Christ is named while tired. Matthew 11:28 holds that weariness honestly and directs its gaze toward Jesus, who gives rest rather than demanding a polished account first.

What weight from this long week feels most present as you sit before Jesus?
